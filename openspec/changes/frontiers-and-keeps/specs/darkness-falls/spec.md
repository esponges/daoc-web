## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Entry follows keeps
Darkness Falls SHALL be open to the realm that holds the most frontier keeps. When ownership changes so that another realm leads, entry SHALL switch, and players of the realm losing access SHALL be moved out after a grace time.

#### Scenario: Losing access
- **WHEN** Albion overtakes Midgard in keeps held while Midgard players are in Darkness Falls
- **THEN** the Midgard players are warned and moved out after the grace time
