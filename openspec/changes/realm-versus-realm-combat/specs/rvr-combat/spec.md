## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Where realms fight
Players of different realms SHALL be able to harm each other only in RvR areas: the frontier zones and battlegrounds. Elsewhere attacks between them SHALL be refused.

#### Scenario: In a home zone
- **WHEN** an enemy player is targeted in a home zone (if one could be there)
- **THEN** attacks are refused

#### Scenario: In the frontier
- **WHEN** a Midgard player attacks an Albion player in a frontier zone
- **THEN** the attack resolves under the player-versus-player rules

### Requirement: Player defences
An attack on a player SHALL roll a hit location and use the armour on that slot, with the player's evade, parry, block and resists.

#### Scenario: Head shot
- **WHEN** a swing lands on the head of a player with a plate helm
- **THEN** the helm's armour factor and absorption reduce the damage

### Requirement: Crowd-control immunity
After a mesmerize, stun or root on a player ends, that player SHALL be immune to the same kind for the rule book's immunity time.

#### Scenario: Stun immunity
- **WHEN** a player's stun ends and another stun is cast on them at once
- **THEN** the second stun has no effect and the caster reads that the target is immune

### Requirement: Death in RvR
A player killed by an enemy realm SHALL lose no experience and no constitution. They SHALL read who killed them, and SHALL release to their realm's frontier or battleground release point.

#### Scenario: Released
- **WHEN** a player killed in the frontier releases
- **THEN** they appear at their realm's frontier release point, with their experience unchanged
