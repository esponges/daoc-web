## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Resurrection returns part of the death's experience
A character who is resurrected instead of releasing SHALL get back the share of the experience lost at death that the rule gives. They SHALL lose no constitution and leave no gravestone. Status: set by the findings.

#### Scenario: Raised at level 20
- **WHEN** a level 20 character who lost experience at death is resurrected
- **THEN** they regain the share the rule book's worked example gives, and no gravestone is made

### Requirement: Resurrection sickness
A resurrected character SHALL carry resurrection sickness for the time the rule gives, lowering their stats or effectiveness by the rule's amount. It SHALL show in their effects list. Status: set by the findings.

#### Scenario: Sickness shows
- **WHEN** a character is resurrected
- **THEN** their effects list shows resurrection sickness with its remaining time

### Requirement: Waiting for a resurrection
A dead character SHALL be able to wait for a resurrection for as long as the rule allows before release is forced. While they wait, they SHALL still be able to release. A pending offer SHALL lapse when they release. Status: set by the findings.

#### Scenario: Release while an offer is pending
- **WHEN** a dead character has an offer and releases instead
- **THEN** the offer lapses and the release costs what it always does
