## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Supply merchants
The merchants that sell crafting materials in the community data SHALL sell them: bars, cloth, leather, wood, gems and herbs. Materials SHALL stack.

#### Scenario: Buying bars
- **WHEN** a Weaponcrafter buys 20 copper bars from a smith's supply merchant
- **THEN** a stack of 20 copper bars is in the bag and the price is paid
