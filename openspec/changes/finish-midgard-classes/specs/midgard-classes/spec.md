## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every Midgard class line works
Every spell type and ability that a Midgard class's data grants up to level 50 SHALL either be implemented or carry a recorded reason in the parity census.

#### Scenario: Savage
- **WHEN** a Savage trains Savagery and opens the Spells window
- **THEN** each spell listed can be cast and has its effect

### Requirement: Midgard Rogue trainer
The Midgard Rogue trainer SHALL serve as the other base-class trainers do: it trains a Midgard Rogue and promotes one at level 5 to Shadowblade or Hunter.

#### Scenario: Promotion
- **WHEN** a level 5 Midgard Rogue speaks to the Rogue trainer
- **THEN** they are offered the classes the start table allows them
