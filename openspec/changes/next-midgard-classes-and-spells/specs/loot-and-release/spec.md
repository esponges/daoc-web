## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Loot by creature level and kind
A kill SHALL pay coin and, with a chance that grows with the creature's level and kind, an item whose level lies within a band of the creature's level and whose kind fits the creature (a humanoid's gear, an animal's hides and parts). Named creatures that have drops in the data SHALL keep those. The table SHALL be marked provisional in the rule book until a source for 1.65's generators is adopted.

#### Scenario: Ordinary kills
- **WHEN** a character kills twenty level 5 creatures of a humanoid kind
- **THEN** the rule's chance of an item per kill is met on average and each item is within the level band

#### Scenario: A named creature
- **WHEN** a named creature with drops in the data is killed
- **THEN** its drops are rolled as before
